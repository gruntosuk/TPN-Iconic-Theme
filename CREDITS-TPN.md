# Iconic TPN - credits and sources

This is a personal adaptation of Iconic for the Team Pixel Nostalgia (PixN) RetroBat build.

## Base theme
- Iconic (ES version) by Delgan - https://github.com/Delgan/iconic-es - CC0-1.0
- Based on Iconic (ES-DE) by Siddy212; see README.md for the full list of original artists.

## Art salvaged from PixN themes (for systems Iconic did not cover)
- Hypermax-Plus-PixN (Team Pixel Nostalgia): background layers (without the Hypermax frame/character overlay) for 60 systems.
  Licence not stated - permission from the PixN team needed before any public release.
- ckau-book-PixN (ckau / Team Pixel Nostalgia): logos for 56 systems and artwork for 14 systems.
  Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 - credit required, non-commercial, share-alike.

Per-system sources are listed in _tools/salvage_log.tsv.

## TPN adaptation
- System-name mapping for the PixN build: _tools/tpn_map.py and _tools/tpn_apply_map.py
